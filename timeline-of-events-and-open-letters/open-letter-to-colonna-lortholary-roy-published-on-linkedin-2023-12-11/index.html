The open letter to Catherine Colonna, Bertrand Lortholary and Guillaume Roy is published on LinkedIn

2023-12-11 2 min read

On December 11, 2023 – 15:05 (China Standard Time, GMT+8), I published on LinkedIn the open letter I had sent one hour and twelve minutes earlier to French Foreign Affairs Minister Catherine Colonna, Ambassador to China Bertrand Lortholary and Consul General Guillaume Roy.

The post read:

I am publicly sharing an open letter addressed to French Foreign Affairs Minister Catherine Colonna, Ambassador to China Bertrand Lortholary, and Consul General Guillaume ROY. This letter concerns a deeply serious matter: the possibility of being blackmailed in the ongoing criminal RICO case against McDonald’s Corporation. While the letter does not delve into the intricate details of the case, it succinctly presents the core issue – my concerns regarding potential blackmail – and the need for urgent attention from these officials. For reasons of privacy and safety, I have redacted parts of the letter. This measure ensures the protection of sensitive information and the safety of all involved parties, while still clearly conveying the critical nature of the situation. Bringing this issue to light is vital for transparency and to seek the appropriate intervention from relevant authorities. I appreciate the community’s attention to this significant matter, as it pertains not just to my personal situation but also to broader questions of corporate conduct and legal accountability. #McDonalds #whitecollarcrime #MoneyLaundering #McDonaldsBankruptcy
