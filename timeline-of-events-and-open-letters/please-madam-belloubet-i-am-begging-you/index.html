Dear Madam Minister,

I sent you a request last week. I implore you, please give me an answer, even if it’s negative. But please say something.

I understand that you must be very busy. You don’t need to respond to me personally. You can delegate this matter to someone else. But please have someone contact me by email quickly. I AM BEGGING YOU, Madam Minister.

I will publicly notify you on the websites NicoleBelloubet.fr and NicoleBelloubet.com that I have created for this purpose. I will make it very clear that these are non-official websites.

Some of the issues I am raising are serious and critical and deserve special attention.

Please accept, Madam, my distinguished greetings.

Vincent Le Corre
